Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 12, 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Brenes QuickBooks Integration ("the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our application to integrate with QuickBooks Online.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 QuickBooks Data

When you authorize the App to connect to your QuickBooks Online account, we access:

  • Customer information
  • Invoice data
  • Account information
  • Transaction records
  • Other financial data you authorize us to access

2.2 Authentication Information

We store:

  • OAuth access tokens (encrypted)
  • OAuth refresh tokens (encrypted)
  • QuickBooks Company ID (Realm ID)

2.3 Technical Information

We may collect:

  • Error logs for troubleshooting
  • API usage statistics

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Provide the App's core functionality
  • Retrieve and display your QuickBooks data
  • Perform automated analysis and reporting
  • Maintain and improve the App
  • Troubleshoot issues and provide support

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

Authentication tokens are stored locally on your computer in encrypted format. We do not transmit your credentials to any external servers beyond QuickBooks API endpoints.

4.2 Security Measures

  • All API communications use HTTPS encryption
  • Tokens are stored using industry-standard encryption
  • We follow QuickBooks API security best practices
  • Access tokens automatically expire and refresh

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or share your QuickBooks data with third parties.

Your data is only shared with:

  • QuickBooks/Intuit: To retrieve and sync your data through their official API
  • No other parties: Your data remains private

6. Data Retention

We retain:

  • Authentication tokens: Until you disconnect the App or tokens expire
  • QuickBooks data: Only during active sessions; not stored long-term
  • Logs: Temporarily for troubleshooting purposes only

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Disconnect the App from QuickBooks at any time
  • Revoke API access through QuickBooks settings
  • Delete locally stored tokens
  • Request information about what data we access

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Compliance

We comply with:

  • Intuit/QuickBooks API Terms of Service
  • Applicable data protection laws
  • Industry security standards

10. Data Breaches

In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting your information, we will:

  • Notify you promptly
  • Take immediate steps to secure your data
  • Comply with applicable breach notification laws

11. International Data Transfers

Your data is processed in the United States. By using the App, you consent to the transfer and processing of your data in the United States.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact the app developer.

14. Your California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to request information about data collection and the right to request deletion of your data.
